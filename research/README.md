The per-domain files and final report will be created during the approved research. This index does not imply those deliverables already exist. Research findings will be dated, cited and separated from vendor claims, user reports and analyst judgment. Product feature coverage will distinguish confirmed support, partial support, confirmed absence and unknown evidence.

The current prototype's narrower Moodle-grading strategy is historical baseline evidence. The present research evaluates the user's broader Whiteboard university-platform vision without presuming the outcome.
