## Evidence gaps

- No public, comparable pricing was found for Engage, CampusGroups, Involve, Suitable, Pathify or Modo.
- No defensible product-level market-share series was found; all market-share fields are blank rather than inferred from vendor customer counts.
- Public sources did not resolve ticketing for Engage, Involve or Suitable; room scheduling for Pathify; or current release status of Suitable secure elections.
- No authenticated product sandbox, contract, implementation statement of work or institution-wide telemetry was available. Reliability and workflow timing therefore remain acceptance-test questions.
