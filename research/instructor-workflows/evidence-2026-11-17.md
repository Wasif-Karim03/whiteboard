# Instructor workflows: evidence and research synthesis

Checked 17 November 2026. Scope: higher-education instructor access to coursework, submissions, grades and teaching-team workflows. Primary sources only: product documentation and published university guidance. Public read-only browsing; no accounts, paid research API, private data uploads or product changes.

This is a selected comparison, not a worldwide survey, market-share study, usability experiment or legal opinion. US examples are George Mason, Columbia Business School and Carnegie Mellon; international examples are UCL (UK), Melbourne (Australia) and Helsinki (Finland). These illustrate variation rather than national rules. Vendor features depend on product version, institution settings, integrations and granted permissions. Undated means no reliable publication/revision date was established; crawl dates were not substituted for publication dates.

## Findings that affect the design

**Two connected views are needed.** Canvas documents class-wide and individual grade views; Blackboard documents opening a student's course record from several entry points. Whiteboard therefore needs both an assessment-to-submissions path and a student-to-coursework path. The latter should be one course's authorized learning record, not an unrestricted institutional dossier. [E01](https://community.instructure.com/en/instructure/kb/articles/662755-what-are-grades-and-the-gradebook), [E03](https://help.anthology.com/blackboard/instructor/en/interact-with-students/student-overview.html).

**Solo grading is a complete path, not a failed team setup.** Moodle describes direct instructor grading before optional division among markers. Blackboard describes delegated graders' restricted contribution. Our recommendation is that Whiteboard's lead instructor retains responsibility for every submitted work unit; a TA allocation changes who can work on a subset, not whether the instructor can see or grade it. This is a design inference, reinforced by the local no-TA probes—not a claim that every vendor implements the same ownership model. [E05](https://docs.moodle.org/405/en/Using_Assignment), [E04](https://help.anthology.com/blackboard/instructor/en/grading/types-of-grading/delegated-grading.html).

**Role titles are not enough.** The US and Australian examples distinguish assistants by capability, section and institutional role. Store authority as explicit grants and scope. Whiteboard's existing TA prohibition on release remains; external documentation does not authorize changing it. Future co-instructor or moderator authority needs a separate explicit decision. [E09](https://its.gmu.edu/knowledge-base/canvas-user-roles-and-adding-users-to-courses/), [E10](https://business.columbia.edu/samberg/cbs-classroom/course-support/course-administration-faqs-v2), [E11](https://lms.unimelb.edu.au/staff/guides/canvas/user-management/staff-roles), [E08](https://community.d2l.com/brightspace/kb/articles/4477-grades-permissions).

**Assessment setup, submission, evaluation and release are different state dimensions.** Keep opening/due/cutoff distinct; distinguish unsent drafts from accepted submissions; show evaluation progress without claiming student visibility. Whiteboard should retain its explicit release boundary even though some systems support automatic posting. [E06](https://docs.moodle.org/405/en/Assignment_settings), [E02](https://community.instructure.com/en/instructure/kb/articles/660845-how-do-i-select-a-grade-posting-policy-for-an-assignment-in-the-gradebook).

**Marking policy must be adaptable without forcing every institution into the most complex case.** UCL supplies a concrete need for independent marks and moderation; D2L documents calculation choices; CMU explains criteria-based grading. Recommended sequence: reliable rubric/manual grading and stated calculation rules first, optional institution-approved moderation later. Do not silently average conflicting marks or change already released outcomes. [E12](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/study/current-students/academic-manual/chapters/chapter-4-assessment-framework-taught-programmes/section-4-marking-moderation), [E07](https://community.d2l.com/brightspace/kb/articles/5196-set-up-your-grade-book), [E14](https://www.cmu.edu/teaching/assessment/assesslearning/rubrics.html).

**LMS release is not necessarily the official academic record.** Helsinki explicitly requires a further SIS confirmation step. Whiteboard should distinguish a course's working grade, student-released result and registrar-accepted final result, and select one actual campus integration before claiming final-grade submission. [E13](https://teaching.helsinki.fi/instructions/article/exporting-grades-moodle-sisu).

**“See everything” must mean everything needed within authorized courses.** Instructors should see the records needed for their authorized courses; TAs should see only their assigned task scope, and learners must not open peers' private course records. None of these views should expose unrelated courses, private calendars, unsent local files or medical diagnoses. Show effective assessment adjustments without copying underlying sensitive evidence into the grader. The data-minimization recommendation follows the stated access purpose; production legal/privacy review remains necessary. [E15](https://studentprivacy.ed.gov/faq/what-must-educational-agencies-or-institutions-do-ensure-only-school-officials-legitimate).

## Boundaries on the recommendations

No adoption percentages, performance gains, representative user findings or country-wide mandates are claimed. No instructor interviews or observed real-campus tasks were conducted for this pass. Before implementation, validate this plan with a solo instructor, a course lead with several graders, a TA, and the chosen institution's registrar/LMS/accessibility staff. Different disciplines, assessment formats, institutional rules and campus systems may change priority. The existing [LMS baseline](../01_lms/notes.md) and [integration authority analysis](../04_cross_cutting/integrations_standards.md) provide history; their version-specific claims need rechecking before engineering.
