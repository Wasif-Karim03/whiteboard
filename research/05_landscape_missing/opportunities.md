# Campus landscape opportunities and product boundaries

Analyst recommendations, 2026-10-15. Evidence: [notes](notes.md), [ranking](priority_ranking.csv), [sources](sources.md). These are hypotheses to validate with first-school buyers, not measured market opportunity sizes.

## Build now within the LMS

- One task object with a responsible person, due/close dates, source, completion rule and safe deep link. Coursework, alerts and later service requests should not produce conflicting parallel to-do lists.
- Consistent course navigation and notification preferences; mobile web completion of real submissions and feedback.
- Optional attendance with transparent correction and permissions. Do not equate a login, booked seat or page view with physical attendance or learning.
- Operational accommodations in assessment, with restricted underlying documentation and institution-defined authority.

The evidence basis is fragmented journeys and inconsistent data meaning in [EDUCAUSE's interoperability guide](https://www.educause.edu/content/2026/the-integrated-institution-a-guide-to-interoperability-for-student-success), existing portal competition in [Pathify](https://pathify.com/), and the local [prototype baseline](../00_baseline/current_build.md).

## Add later after a successful teaching pilot

1. Transparent student-support referral: who owns it, whether it was received, and what the student must do next. Domain 06 establishes the full advising market and boundaries.
2. Event/room coordination using authoritative confirmations. Never show a requested room as booked merely because an event was approved.
3. Evaluation status and learning-evidence export. Protect survey anonymity, explain sample limits, and preserve student permissions and post-graduation access choices.
4. A campus portal/native app only when measured discovery, offline or device needs justify it. Native delivery is not itself proof of a better service.

Nearby evidence: [Watermark](https://www.watermarkinsights.com/), [Digication](https://www.digication.com/), [Ready Education](https://www.readyeducation.com/), [NC State evaluation cautions](https://provost.ncsu.edu/ofe/teaching-learning/evaluation-of-teaching-at-nc-state/classeval-faq/).

## Integrate for the foreseeable roadmap

Keep official student/degree records, aid/accounting/payroll, clinical records, conduct narratives, housing allocation, parking enforcement, library collections, payments/IDs/door access and employer marketplaces authoritative in their specialist systems. Offer permission-scoped status, referral, approved contextual actions and export. “One platform” should mean predictable work and connected information rather than an assumption of universal record ownership.

The specialist scope is documented by [Workday](https://www.workday.com/en-us/solutions/industries/education.html), [Illumia](https://illumiatech.com/), [Titanium](https://www.titaniumschedule.com/Main/), [Maxient](https://www.maxient.com/), [StarRez](https://www.starrez.com/), [OCLC](https://www.oclc.org/en/worldshare-management-services.html), and [Handshake](https://joinhandshake.com/employers/).

## Cut or avoid

- A universal campus CRM, workflow builder or financial ledger in the LMS MVP.
- Automatic guilt or risk labels based on weak behavioral signals.
- Shared access to sensitive records merely because accounts use one SSO.
- Unverified claims that all named suppliers are outdated, that every campus wants one vendor, or that a single subscription guarantees lower total cost.

## Acceptance tests and discovery priorities

- Student sees the same task status in course, Today, calendar and notification destination.
- An external service fails: source freshness, pending status, recovery and owner remain visible.
- A referral exposes only required operational context; unrelated faculty cannot retrieve a case narrative.
- Survey completion is actionable without disclosing a named student's answers to instructors.
- Institution exports its teaching records and verifies permission, rubric and grade fidelity.
- Ask a small college, a community college and a university which adjacent service is truly worth consolidating, who controls its budget and which incumbent they would actually retire.

Largest risks: broader scope outruns support capacity; the unified interface conceals stale data; sensitive records cross an improper boundary; established suite/portal suppliers already solve enough of the problem; campus staff prefer depth and continuity over vendor consolidation. No savings, adoption or retention improvement is assumed before a measured pilot.
