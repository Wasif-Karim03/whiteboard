# The campus software landscape Whiteboard still needs to account for

As of 15 October 2026. This is a broad boundary and opportunity scan, with six portfolio deep dives. It is not a claim to have enumerated every supplier in every campus vertical. The [31-domain ranking](priority_ranking.csv) records representative suppliers, pain, ability to win, LMS proximity and a now/later/never decision. Component scores are subjective 1–5 product judgments; their product is a screening aid, not measured demand or market size. [Sources](sources.md) distinguish product claims, institutional guidance, user reports and uncertainty.

## The important distinction: experience and authority

An institution can give a student one coherent place to act while retaining separate authoritative systems. EDUCAUSE's September 2026 interoperability guide describes fragmentation, inconsistent definitions and weak handoffs, then recommends governance and phased integration. That supports Whiteboard's shared experience goal, but does not prove that replacing every underlying application is the best remedy. [LAND-01](https://www.educause.edu/content/2026/the-integrated-institution-a-guide-to-interoperability-for-student-success).

The proposed product boundary is: own teaching workflows and their records; progressively own suitable campus coordination workflows; connect to official student, financial, personnel, clinical and physical-access systems. “Never” in the ranking means outside the foreseeable Whiteboard product roadmap, not a prediction that the company can never enter that market.

## Six competitor and portfolio deep dives

### Pathify: the competing unified experience

Pathify presents a campus hub with tasks, announcements, forms and communities over connected systems. Its current site names middleware and institutional governance alongside the user interface. Vendor-selected University of the Pacific and Montgomery County Community College stories describe consolidation of campus-facing experiences. These are relevant references, not independently verified causal savings. [LAND-02](https://pathify.com/).

Its strength is that it can sell coherence without requiring a full LMS or SIS replacement. Its limitation for our comparison is the same boundary: the source systems still determine record correctness, freshness and permitted actions. No public comparable institution-wide price was verified. Whiteboard must demonstrate task completion and reliable shared state; a dashboard containing many links is not enough. See Domain 03 for the deeper Communities feature and pain review.

### Ready Education: campus reach and student communities

Ready Education markets campus mobile and engagement offerings, including CampusGroups. This is a practical alternative for schools whose immediate problem is student-life reach and discovery rather than teaching authoring. The wider family should not be treated as one identically packaged product. [LAND-03](https://www.readyeducation.com/), [Domain 03](../03_events_clubs/notes.md).

Strength: mobile/community scope and the documented CampusGroups organization workflows. Open questions: institution-specific packaging, real adoption, authoritative directory maintenance, integration direction and cost. The opportunity for Whiteboard is to connect a student task to the underlying course/event action with consistent identity and completion status. A new native application alone does not establish that advantage. Numeric pricing and ultimate ownership are not established by the landscape source.

### Watermark: the academic evidence business beyond the gradebook

Watermark's current portfolio spans evaluations and surveys, curriculum, learning/licensure evidence, faculty activity, planning/accreditation and student success. Its site also points to Canvas integration. This makes it a nearby institutional competitor even though it is not a substitute LMS. [LAND-21](https://www.watermarkinsights.com/).

Strength: domain-specific evidence and reporting workflows that a course-grade table does not provide. Limitation: these are separate product and governance scopes; the portfolio page does not establish that every function is included in one contract. Public numeric prices were not verified. Whiteboard should provide clear outcomes/export interfaces first. Course evaluation anonymity, response bias, retention and faculty-review policy require separate design. NC State cautions against overinterpreting online ratings, so a clean survey cannot be sold as an unbiased measure of teaching quality. [LAND-26](https://provost.ncsu.edu/ofe/teaching-learning/evaluation-of-teaching-at-nc-state/classeval-faq/).

### Symplicity: specialized student services and careers

Symplicity's current higher-ed positioning spans career and student-service operations. Its homepage includes a Penn State career-center account and a Hartnell disability-service story. This establishes relevant use cases; it does not establish a uniform single platform entitlement or quantified outcomes. [LAND-08](https://www.symplicity.com/higher-ed), [LAND-09](https://www.symplicity.com/).

Career operations involve employers, opportunities, appointments and placement processes. Handshake's employer-facing recruiting network illustrates a different asset: market participation that cannot be replicated by adding a jobs table. [LAND-07](https://joinhandshake.com/employers/). Whiteboard should surface approved opportunities and appointment/status handoffs before attempting a career marketplace. Disability services should share operational accommodations with teaching staff only under institutional rules; sensitive underlying case records need restricted ownership. No comparable public institutional price or ultimate parent was verified here.

### Illumia: campus commerce and physical identity

Illumia joins campus credentials/access, dining/POS, student payments and related integrations. This is an example of a genuinely connected campus domain with physical and financial responsibilities. The official page explicitly describes ERP integration and payment security as part of the scope. [LAND-04](https://illumiatech.com/), [transition/parent evidence](https://illumiatech.com/about/becoming-illumia).

Strength: depth around credentials, transactions and physical operations. Limitation for Whiteboard: adding balances, meal plans or event entry creates reconciliation and availability responsibilities beyond a campus calendar. We should integrate authorized balances/status and deep links when a pilot requires them. Stored-value accounts, payment processing, door access and meal-plan accounting stay outside the initial product. No numeric institutional list price is claimed. Vendor global usage claims are deliberately excluded from our US higher-ed market estimates.

### Workday: the institutional system-of-record boundary

Workday's education offering connects student administration, finance, workforce and planning. It competes for broad institutional transformation and supplies records that an LMS must interpret correctly. [LAND-19](https://www.workday.com/en-us/solutions/industries/education.html), [Domain 04](../04_cross_cutting/notes.md).

Strength: official institutional operations across several offices. Limitations: implementation, authority and integration need exact object/version/permission definitions. This scan does not establish a comparable public price or universal ease of use. Whiteboard's roadmap should exchange roster/section/grade data and show reconciliation; it should not quietly become a new official transcript, payroll or student-aid ledger. Populi is a different unified-suite counterexample for smaller colleges, showing why segment-specific comparison matters. [LAND-30](https://www.populi.co/features/).

## Other important boundaries

Housing is more than room booking: StarRez documents roommate selection, resident billing, residence life, maintenance and conference lodging. Library systems include collection, acquisitions and circulation processes, as shown by OCLC WorldShare and the Alma/Primo selection at Yale. Parking adds permits and enforcement. These are legitimate campus needs with low initial Whiteboard fit. [StarRez](https://www.starrez.com/), [OCLC](https://www.oclc.org/en/worldshare-management-services.html), [Clarivate/Yale](https://clarivate.com/news/yale-university-selects-clarivate-to-provide-their-next-library-services-and-discovery-platforms/), [T2](https://www.t2systems.com/).

Clinical and conduct software deserve explicit separation. Titanium documents clinical notes, restricted access, accommodations and an institution-hosted Windows application; Maxient specializes in conduct cases. A shared sign-in must not imply shared visibility of the underlying narratives. These are product-boundary judgments, not assertions that every campus record falls under the same law. Applicable access/retention requirements need institutional review. [Titanium](https://www.titaniumschedule.com/Main/), [Maxient](https://www.maxient.com/), [cross-cutting requirements](../04_cross_cutting/compliance_security.md).

Help desks can be connected through request/status handoffs. TeamDynamix's ITSM/ESM and project/asset scope shows why rebuilding the full service organization would be a large diversion. Alumni and admissions have their own relationship workflows in Blackbaud and Slate; the LMS can supply approved transitions and exports. [TeamDynamix](https://www.teamdynamix.com/industries/higher-education/), [Blackbaud](https://www.blackbaud.com/), [Slate](https://technolutions.com/).

## Feature matrix and real pain

The [48-cell matrix](feature_matrix.csv) compares eight portfolio scopes across six deep dives. ✅ means the opened evidence establishes that advertised scope. Partial means narrower scope or reliance on connected records. Unknown means unestablished; it does not mean absent. Portfolio coverage is not a ranking of depth, product quality or implementation ease.

The [pain register](pain_points.csv) distinguishes actual user testimony from sector guidance and documented limitations. Examples include fragmented support handoffs, ambiguous data meanings, course-evaluation nonresponse/bias, privacy objections to room-video proctoring, service-ticket ownership and explicit clinical-device/migration constraints. Severity is our assessment of consequence, not a measured frequency. The proctoring thread contains mixed views and is not a verdict on a current product version. [EDUCAUSE](https://www.educause.edu/content/2026/the-integrated-institution-a-guide-to-interoperability-for-student-success), [NC State](https://provost.ncsu.edu/ofe/teaching-learning/evaluation-of-teaching-at-nc-state/classeval-faq/), [faculty discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/Professors/comments/1hxpsy9/honorlock_and_respondus_what_say_thee/).

Individual testimony is thinner for remote campus verticals than for LMS, rooms and events. The ranking does not pretend all 31 categories have equal research depth. Scores map high/medium/low judgments to 5/3/1. Changing any component by one can change the order among nearby opportunities; implementation burden, trust risk and actual buyer access should overrule small score differences. Rows retain the editorial research order, not a claim of precise numerical ranking. Lecture capture, emergency communications and noncredit-specialist competitors require follow-up; blocked vendor pages were not used as verified evidence. There is no defensible landscape-wide market share, public comparable price range or quantitative pain prevalence in this scan.

## Promotion and recommendation

Promote **advising, degree planning and student success** into [Domain 06](../06_student_success_advising/notes.md). They are close to course progress and support handoffs, with clear institutional suppliers. Research them fully, but sequence development after the teaching record is dependable. The first suitable feature is an explainable referral with owner/status and minimal necessary information, not an opaque risk score or unofficial degree audit. [Ellucian](https://www.ellucian.com/en-ae/products/student/student-success), [EAB](https://eab.com/solutions/navigate360/), [EDUCAUSE](https://www.educause.edu/content/2026/the-integrated-institution-a-guide-to-interoperability-for-student-success).

For the LMS MVP, own accessible coursework, optional transparent attendance, tasks/notifications and operational accommodations. Later, test course feedback/evaluations, portfolios, support referrals and selected event/room coordination. Keep specialist financial, clinical, physical-access and official-record systems integration-led. This is a roadmap recommendation, not a claim that all these modules should ship in the first release.

## Comparison to the build

The [baseline](../00_baseline/current_build.md) contains none of the production campus services above. Today/calendar/course concepts are design evidence, not an integrated portal, attendance engine, adviser case system or registration system. Reuse the visual task/calendar direction; implement common identity, authorization, persistent records and a documented event/status model before adding service breadth. Cut unsupported “whole university already works” claims and avoid counting prototype controls as completed features.
