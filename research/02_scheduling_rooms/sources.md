## Interpretation rules

- A vendor page can support “the vendor documents this capability,” not an outcome claim.
- A public contract can support an exact amount only for that buyer, term, and scope.
- A review or forum post can identify a plausible failure mode. It cannot establish frequency.
- Customer counts are kept separate from market share. No reliable comparable market-share series was found, so market-share fields are blank.
- “Unknown” in the feature matrix means the reviewed public evidence did not establish the function. It does not mean the function is absent.
- Ownership descriptions use the most specific official wording available. CollegeNET's official announcement says “majority investment,” while Ellucian's official announcement says its Anthology SIS/ERP acquisition was completed on 2025-12-31.
