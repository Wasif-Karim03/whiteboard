# Buying, economics and migration

As of 2026-10-15. Prices below are deliberately scoped. No private quote was requested, paid service invoked or commitment made. A contract amount is not a general market price.

## Who participates

This is a discovery hypothesis, not a universal organization chart. At District 532, a CIO is the named sponsor of the cited subscription recommendation; San Juan's minutes show purchasing and board involvement. [CROSS-S37, CROSS-S36]

## Real procurement evidence

Delaware State University's 2026 academic-management RFP is an adjacent-platform example, **not an LMS award**. Its evaluation allocates 20 points to cost, 15 to implementation and 10 to documentation, within a 100-point scheme. It calls for HECVAT, VPAT, API and contract documentation, integrations, training and migration; a price sheet separates recurring and implementation categories. Bids were due June9 with a projected August1 start, subject to change. This planned window is not evidence of actual delivery speed. [CROSS-S35]

For Whiteboard, request institution-specific milestones: budget submission, RFP/sole-source/cooperative route, evaluation and accessibility review, contracting, integration freeze, faculty training and term start. Do not promise a standard sales cycle from one notice. A pilot department's discretionary budget does not automatically authorize institution-wide educational-record processing.

## Commercial proposal for a first school (recommendation)

Separate subscription, implementation, connector work, migration, training, support, storage/retention, AI usage and exit assistance. Define billable student/active user/FTE/seat precisely; handle cross-registration, summer and non-credit learners. State module/tenant limits, renewal escalation, minimums, overages, support hours, SLA exclusions, data export rights, termination and archive access.

Prepare an evidence packet with architecture/data flow, security questionnaire, tested ACR, data processing terms, subprocessors, retention, incident process, backup/restore evidence and migration acceptance plan. A small vendor may propose a bounded pilot with explicit exceptions; the buyer decides whether residual risk is acceptable. Do not imply that a report absent today will exist by signing without a funded, realistic plan.

## Switching and exit risk

Institutional evidence shows migration is not merely choosing an export format. [CROSS-S38, CROSS-S39] Budget campus time for catalog/course mapping, faculty remediation, gradebook policy checks, accessible content, historical records and training. Test the integration during a term rollover and restore from a failed migration. Separate pilot success criteria from general rollout acceptance.

Offer a verifiable exit path from the first release: documented exports, downloadable originals/feedback, protected audit records, deletion confirmation, a transition window and predictable assistance fees. Our inference is that reduced switching risk could make the purchasing decision easier; no buyer willingness-to-pay evidence for that feature was collected.
