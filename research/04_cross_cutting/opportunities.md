# Opportunities and readiness gates

All prioritization below is analyst judgment as of 2026-10-15. Desk evidence identifies constraints and plausible opportunities; it does not establish demand, willingness to pay or causal time savings.

## Where Whiteboard could win

**Make correctness understandable.** Expose source, last sync, status, pending work and recoverable failure at the point of action. In a grading workflow, users should distinguish saved, reviewed, released and acknowledged by the LMS/SIS. This directly addresses the prototype's disconnected states and turns integration reliability into visible user value. Institutional migration accounts and integration cases motivate the hypothesis. [CROSS-S21, CROSS-S38, CROSS-S39]

**Reduce institution-side setup and migration work.** Offer a scoped connector contract, a preflight report, field-level errors, golden-course comparison and faculty acceptance. This is more defensible than promising every existing course imports perfectly. Validate by observing actual staff time and exceptions in an authorized pilot; no savings figure is yet justified.

**Make accessibility and trust reviewable.** Build accessible interaction paths, dated evidence and remediable exceptions into delivery. A clean interface can still be inaccessible, and an administration screen can still lack real controls. Procurement/accessibility evidence supports a product requirement, not a marketing certification claim. [CROSS-S06, CROSS-S07, CROSS-S35]

**Provide a unified task experience across systems.** Align navigation, vocabulary and next actions while preserving authoritative records. This may satisfy the campus-platform ambition without replacing financial aid, payroll or identity systems. Competing integrated suites mean unification itself is not novel; Whiteboard must demonstrate better task completion and continuity in the selected segment.

**Use AI where reviewable assistance improves a proven task.** Incumbent availability makes generic AI authoring a weak standalone differentiator. Candidate experiments include draft feedback with source anchors or submission-quality guidance, gated by local evidence, cost authorization and faculty acceptance. [CROSS-S40–CROSS-S48]

## Pilot, institution and expansion are different promises

| Gate | Must be true | Smallest useful evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Design/research prototype | Use synthetic data; clearly label concepts. | Working prototype tasks and honest limitation notes; no compliance/customer claims. |
| Real-data teaching-workflow pilot | Institution sponsor and data terms; supported identity/LMS path; server permissions and tenant isolation; persistent submissions/grades; accessible critical tasks; backup/incident/support ownership; safe export. | End-to-end course scenario including revoked TA, interrupted upload, grade-sync failure, restore and accessible feedback. |
| Full-LMS pilot | Above plus complete participating-course continuity: authoring, assessment, grade policy, notifications, materials, accommodations and course records. | Golden-course migration and semester workflow with faculty acceptance and fallbacks. |
| Institution-wide LMS contract | Procurement evidence; full role/term/SIS operations; migration/training; support and release management; load/recovery tests; retention/archive/exit; buyer-required assurance. | Rehearsed term rollover, mixed course acceptance, realistic peak scenarios, restore test and evidence review. |
| Cross-campus expansion | Explicit tenant/campus boundary, cross-registration rules, federation, local calendars/policy, adapter versioning and operations. | Two distinct institution configurations with negative access tests and independent data/grade reconciliation. |

No operational service in these gates is proven by the audited HTML. Reuse the visual vocabulary, role concepts and recovery-state designs; repair release/receipt/navigation defects and build the underlying services. A credible MVP is the smallest complete authorized learning workflow, not the largest collection of screens.

## Build, integrate and defer

**Build now:** coherent course/assessment data, permissions, submission/grade state, accessible workflow and observability. **Integrate now when the partner requires it:** one institution identity provider, one LMS tool interface and necessary SIS feed. **Prepare before institution-wide sale:** evidence pack, portable records, migration acceptance, support and independent evaluation. **Defer until validated:** broad connector catalogs, cross-campus identity brokerage, analytics standards without a decision use case, autonomous AI and wide campus-system replacement.

Do not build an IdP, payroll ledger, financial-aid rules engine or a generic iPaaS merely to claim completeness. The wider landscape should decide native module priorities using workflow proximity, trust burden and evidence of persistent pain. An integration can be a finished product feature if it makes the user's whole task reliable.

## Discovery and acceptance agenda

Recruit real institutions across a public university, private college and community-college setting; the existing tracker contains no completed interviews. Validate the current SIS/IdP/LMS contract, who controls money and data, a recent migration failure, actual staff effort, accessibility barriers and the next renewal window. Ask what would make them decline a pilot, not only what features they want.

Observe the same student/instructor/admin tasks on current tools and Whiteboard. Measure completion, recoverable errors, help needed and effort without inventing a baseline. Include keyboard and assistive-technology participants with appropriate consent. Inspect teaching records only under approved data handling. A single campus success should support a bounded claim, not a market-wide adoption forecast.

## Handoff limits

No legal opinion, external audit, customer interview, connector execution, product trial, AI benchmark or willingness-to-pay test was performed. Prices are scoped primary records; missing numbers stay blank. Source coverage favors public US institutions for procurement transparency and major ecosystems for integration; smaller/private contracts remain a gap. Readiness work should precede production claims in the prototype's marketing copy.
