## Evidence rules

- `unknown` means public evidence reviewed did not establish a capability; it is not an assertion of absence.
- Degree audits are kept distinct from official transcripts, program changes, registrar exceptions, and graduation clearance.
- Current events and observable signals are kept distinct from predicted risk.
- The 850-school / 10-million-student figure belongs to Navigate360 and Starfish together.
- Starfish belongs to EAB. Encoura's 2026 completed acquisition covered Anthology's Lifecycle Engagement and Student Success portfolio, not Starfish.
- Commercial pricing is recorded as quote-based because no comparable current public price card was found. No amount was inferred.
