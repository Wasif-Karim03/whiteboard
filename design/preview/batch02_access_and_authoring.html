Arrival and instructor authoring

Batch 02 reuses the Batch 01 system. It covers how a person arrives — institution sign-in, invitations, first use and account (P41–P44, proposed destinations) — and how an instructor builds what students later see: course builder, assignment and quiz editors (S14–S16, concepts) and lesson pages, course files and rubrics (P47–P49, proposed).

Staff pages use a teaching workspace shell; access pages use a focused shell with no product navigation until identity is known. Links to Batch 01 screens open that page. Add #S15:invalid to the address to open a state directly.

What this is — and isn’t

  • Design review draft. Not owner-accepted.
  • All people, schools, courses, scores and receipts are fictional.
  • Web frames are concept designs; P-numbered screens are proposed destinations. iPhone frames are HTML illustrations of a planned iOS app; no native build exists.
  • No sign-in, identity provider, upload, storage, grading service or live integration exists. URLs use the reserved .example domain.
  • Browser, keyboard, screen-reader and device checks have not been run. Only static source checks have.

Spacing, shape, motion, focus

  • 4-pt grid: 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 20 · 24 · 32 · 40 · 56.
  • Radii: controls 8, inset groups 10, cards 12, sheets 16.
  • Elevation: one soft card shadow; modals add scrim. No gradients.
  • Motion 120–320 ms ease-out for state changes only; none under Reduce Motion.
  • Focus: 3 px blue ring, 2 px offset, on every interactive element.
  • Light/dark from system preference with a manual override; iOS uses true-black grouped backgrounds.

Contrast · static token math

63 token pairs across light, dark and iOS dark: all meet target. Lowest text pair: light 5.34:1, dark 5.96:1, iOS dark 6.41:1.

Colour arithmetic only. Rendered contrast, forced colours and screen-reader output are not yet verified.
