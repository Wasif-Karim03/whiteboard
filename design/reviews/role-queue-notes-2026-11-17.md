# Grading queue record and role consistency

Chosen by Codex under owner delegation: W19 links to a grading queue only when that queue opens the displayed assignment and a TA has an active task for it. An assignment without a drawn queue says so. W48 keeps its anchored assignment's counts and receipt rows together and presents any authorized group receipt in a separately titled assignment section. Each TA task is checked independently, including when another task remains active after a task expires. The group receipt retains its existing grader destination.

Verification is source-level only; browser, keyboard, assistive technology and device behavior remain unverified.
