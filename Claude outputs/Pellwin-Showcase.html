Pellwin design review

One place to judge how Pellwin looks, reads and behaves before we build it. Tier 1 is the grading tool we’re building now. Tier 2 is the wider platform we might build later, shown only so we don’t paint ourselves into a corner.

Full fidelity. Working interactions. Exact wording from Tech-v2 Phase 3b. Screens GT-01 to GT-30, flows FL-GT-01 to FL-GT-14.

Brand

Brand voice

Pellwin talks like a good TA at 11:40 pm: calm, specific and brief. It says what happened, then what to do. Quotes marked “3b” are the exact strings from Tech-v2 Phase 3b.

Five principles

PrincipleWhat it means in practice
Say what happened, then what to doFirst clause is the fact. Second is the fix. No preamble, no apology unless we caused it.
Protect the student’s work firstWhen something fails during a submission, the first thing we say is that their pages are safe.
Name people, not systems“Maya (TA) graded this” beats “A conflict was detected.”
Numbers over adjectives“3 of 41 scores” and “62% sent”, never “some” or “almost done”.
Calm near deadlinesNo exclamation marks, no countdown drama, no cheerleading. Deadlines get the time and the time zone.

Mechanics

  • Sentence case for every label, button and heading.
  • Buttons are verbs: “Release grades”, “Retake”, “Export CSV”.
  • Times always carry a zone near deadlines: “11:59 pm EDT”.
  • Lowercase am and pm, with a space: “9:00 am”.
  • Curly quotes and apostrophes, a real ellipsis (…), a non‑breaking space in “4 pts”.
  • Points: “−2” uses a true minus sign, not a hyphen.
  • Scores: “26 / 30”, tabular figures, thin spaces around the slash.
  • Error text sits next to the thing it’s about and states the fix.
  • No emoji in the product. No exclamation marks in errors.
  • Em dashes are rare. Prefer a full stop or a colon.

DN-36: Material Symbols Rounded on web, SF Symbols on iOS

Icons

One set per platform, never mixed. Outline by default; filled only for a selected tab and for status icons. Every icon here lists its SF Symbol so iOS matches meaning, not pixels.

Rules

  • Web: 20 px on a 20 grid (optical size 20), weight 400. 16 px inside chips.
  • iOS: SF Symbols at the text style’s size, .medium scale in bars.
  • Icons follow text color. Only status icons take a status color.
  • No emoji as icons, anywhere (AA-24).
  • Icon-only buttons need an accessible name and a tooltip on web.
  • Mortarboard (school) is excluded on purpose: EdTech cliché.
