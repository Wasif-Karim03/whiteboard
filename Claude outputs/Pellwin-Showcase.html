One experience, clear boundaries

Keep the right expertise.

An integrated product should make a task easier to complete. Specialist depth, migration and institutional choice still matter.

Different approaches serve different institutional priorities. Whiteboard describes our planned approach.
ApproachWhat it offersWhat still needs care
Specialist stackEstablished tools and depth in individual domains.Cross-system context, contracts and handoffs.
Connected campus portalA common front door over existing systems.The underlying workflows and records remain distributed.

The vision and the path

Build the foundation.
Then bring more together.

The screens describe the intended product. This sequence is our direction, without promised launch dates or claims of live availability.

Core learning

A complete, dependable course cycle: content, assignments, quizzes, submissions, grading and feedback.

Institutional depth

Course migration, term rollover, integrations, accessible workflows, support and operational evidence.

Campus coordination

Room requests, scheduling changes, events and organizations with shared context and clear ownership.

Connected support

Transparent referrals, helpful service discovery and selected campus adjacencies, with authority kept explicit.

What would make this work for your campus?

We want to understand a real teaching workflow, the systems around it and what a careful first pilot would need.

Selected research and context

Research checked 15 October 2026. Institutional examples establish local needs, not national prevalence or measured Whiteboard outcomes.

  1. EDUCAUSE: The Integrated Institution (September 2026) — meaningful data exchange, shared definitions and student-support handoffs.
  2. Villanova: academic scheduling initiative — institution-specific coordination across departments.
  3. Montana State: migration checklist — course/record migration needs deliberate review.
  4. EDUCAUSE: HECVAT and ITI: VPAT/ACR — risk and conformance evidence, not product certifications.
